Long-form text

A multi-page wall of prose. The point of this one is the page joins: text should resume on the next page at a clean line boundary, with the previous page filled to within one line-height of the bottom.

Long-form Text — Line-level Pagination

Scroll to the bottom of each generated page and look at the join. Because every visual line is its own measured box, the last line that fits is the last line placed, and the next page starts with the line right after it.

Section 1

Section 1 exists to make this document long enough to span several pages. Each paragraph here wraps across multiple visual lines, and the analyzer turns each of those lines into its own box before pagination ever sees them. That is the difference between this approach and treating a paragraph as one indivisible unit: an indivisible paragraph that does not fit has to move wholesale to the next page, leaving behind exactly the unexplained white space this project exists to eliminate.

The second paragraph of section 1 repeats the shape of the first, so the document has a steady rhythm of headings and body text. Watch what happens to a heading that lands near the bottom of a page: keepWithNext pulls it down to the next page rather than orphaning it above a break with none of its own text under it.
