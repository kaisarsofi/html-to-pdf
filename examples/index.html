html-to-pdf — examples

Each page below is an ordinary page in this tab. Nothing is hidden, screenshotted, or sent to a server: clicking Preview or Download measures the #content subtree in place, paginates it, and writes a real vector PDF. Every example reports how many boxes were measured and how many pages the engine decided on.

  • Mixed report Headings, wrapped paragraphs and a table — the full pipeline end to end.
  • Long-form text Multi-page prose. Pages fill to within one line-height of the bottom; breaks land on real line boundaries; headings are never orphaned above a break.
  • Long table 60 rows across several pages. <tr> is atomic, so a row that does not fit moves whole rather than being sliced.
  • Lists and keep-together cards <li> is atomic by tag; a plain <div> card opts in with data-pdf-break="avoid".
  • Images and figures PNG and JPEG embedded as real PDF image objects, with captions kept beside them.
  • Fonts Sans, serif and monospace mapped onto PDF's built-in standard 14 for free, plus a real web font embedded straight from the page's own @font-face rule.
  • Oversized content A box taller than a whole page: placed on a fresh page and reported as a warning, never dropped silently.

Serving these

Native ES modules and an import map mean these must be served over HTTP, not opened as file://. From the project root: npx serve .
