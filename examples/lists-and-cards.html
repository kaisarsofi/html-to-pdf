Lists and cards

List items are atomic by tag. Cards are plain <div>s, so they opt in explicitly with data-pdf-break="avoid" — the escape hatch for layouts the analyzer cannot infer.

Cards (atomic by opt-in)

A card is not a tr or an li, so nothing about its tag says "keep me together". The data-pdf-break="avoid" attribute says it instead.
